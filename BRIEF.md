# Fisicora: project brief

## Idea
Fisicora is AI software for physique coaches and nutrition professionals in Europe (Italy first). The AI removes the manual work of running a practice: it listens to consultations and check-ins, writes the client summary, drafts meal and training plans with macros, sends reminders and prepares invoices. The professional reviews and approves; the AI does the paperwork.

## Audience
- Physique and bodybuilding coaches: cut, offseason, diet breaks, peak week, weekly check-ins, progress photos, training splits.
- Nutritionists and dietitians: anamnesis, allergies and intolerances, clinical notes, meal plans, follow-ups.
- Also: gyms and studios, sports teams.

## Brand
- Name: Fisicora (from Italian "fisico", body/physique). Domains free on 15 Oct 2026: fisicora.com, fisicora.it, fisicora.eu (not bought yet). Check EUIPO trademark search before buying.
- Palette: deep ink navy, warm terracotta accent, cream backgrounds, sage support colour.
- Fonts: Fraunces (headings), Inter (body).
- Languages: English and Italian (/en and /it).

## Reference
- Layout and section flow inspired by cibushealth.com/en (hero, how it works in 3 steps, feature tabs, pricing, FAQ, demo request form, wide footer). No text, images or branding copied.
- App demo inspired by the Cibus product walkthrough video (client record with tabs, AI voice transcription, measurements, meal plan with macros, diary).
- Coaching domain inspired by Kuba Cielen (Kilo Culture) and Justin Harris (Troponin Nutrition): diet phases, offseason, check-ins.

## Scope (phase 1)
- Marketing website (EN + IT) with working demo request form (front end only for now).
- Clickable app demo at /en/app and /it/app with demo data and simulated AI (no real AI or database yet).

## Next phases (not built yet)
- Real backend: auth, database, client records, Stripe billing (EU methods), real AI transcription and plan generation.
- Pricing on the site is a placeholder to confirm.
